## Brand & Style

This design system translates the educational ethos, communal spirit, and craftsmanship embodied in the tripartite circular school seal into a modern digital experience. The interface balances educational clarity, creative energy, and practical vocational warmth.

### Design Movement & Core Philosophy
The visual direction blends **Modern Institutional** structure with **Playful Soft Modernism**. It prioritizes:
- **Clarity & Inclusivity:** Accessible typography and high-contrast affordances suitable for students, educators, and parents alike.
- **Organic Geometry:** Rounded container forms, circular motifs, and soft dividing outlines derived directly from the concentric emblem geometry.
- **Empowering Triad:** Layout and visual rhythm reflect the school emblem's tripartite balance: mental cognition (gears & head), collaborative community (helping hands), and applied craftsmanship (technical tools).

## Colors

The color hierarchy directly reflects the school seal's symbolic palette:

- **Primary School Cerulean (`#0B7BA7`):** Represents structural clarity, community, and official institutional communication. Anchors primary navigation, active tabs, header branding, and primary action buttons.
- **Secondary Teal (`#00A896`):** Symbolizes curiosity, intellect, and creativity. Used for progress indicators, success feedback, secondary interactive chips, and creative modules.
- **Tertiary Warm Marigold (`#F39200`):** Extracted directly from the circumferential emblem typography. Brings warmth, energy, and highlights announcements, badges, callouts, and key interaction cues.
- **Neutral Deep Slate (`#2C3E50`):** Provides sharp, accessible contrast for high-legibility body text and dark structural borders without the harshness of pure black.
- **Surface & Tinted Canvas Layers:** Pure crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) serves as the base layer, complemented by tinted soft containers:
  - Blue tint (`#F0F8FA` / `#E6F4F8`) for administrative cards, schedules, and structural segments.
  - Golden tint (`#FEF7EE`) for parent alerts, celebratory events, and critical timetable updates.

## Typography

**Plus Jakarta Sans** is employed across all hierarchy levels. Its geometric foundations and friendly, open apertures mirror the clean, modern letterforms found in the outer circular typography of the school seal.

### Hierarchy & Usage
- **Display & Large Headlines:** Emphasize high-impact school announcements, seasonal greetings, and portal dashboards with semi-bold to extra-bold weights.
- **Headlines & Section Titles:** Clear and inviting, guiding users through subject dashboards, course assignments, and calendar overviews.
- **Body:** Engineered for extended readability across device displays with open line heights (1.5–1.6) ensuring effortless comprehension for younger learners and parents.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Crisp, slightly tracking-expanded weights (`label-sm` and `label-md`) are deployed for timetable chips, status badges, and technical metadata.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column fluid grid system optimized for information density that stays readable and uncluttered.

### Spatial Rhythm
- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, `1rem` outer canvas margin, and `1.25rem` column gutters.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8-column layout, `1.5rem` outer canvas margin, and `1.5rem` gutters.
  - **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout with a maximum container width of `1280px`, `2.5rem` canvas margins, and `2rem` gutters.
- **Component Padding Scale:** Strict increments based on multiples of 4px/8px ensure modular symmetry. Use `space-sm` for compact chip padding, `space-md` for standard button and form field insets, and `space-lg` to `space-xl` for card modules and panel separations.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy drop shadows, opting for clean, luminous school-friendly depth achieved via **tonal layering** and **soft ambient color glows**:

### Depth Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** Crisp base background (`#FFFFFF`) or tinted page canvas (`#F8FBFC`).
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Surface):** Soft background containers (`#F0F8FA` or `#FEF7EE`) paired with a subtle, low-opacity structural border (`rgba(11, 123, 167, 0.12)`).
- **Level 2 (Interactive Elements & Dropdowns):** Subtle ambient tint shadow: `0 6px 20px -4px rgba(11, 123, 167, 0.08)`, offering clear physical lift without visual clutter.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Overlays):** Elevation `0 16px 36px -8px rgba(11, 123, 167, 0.16)` with an ambient background blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px)` with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`).

## Shapes

The shape system honors the circular silhouette and clean interior partitions of the school emblem:
- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Cards):** Standardized at `rounded-md` (0.5rem) and `rounded-lg` (1rem), providing tactile warmth and safe ergonomics.
- **Pill Badges & Filter Chips:** Full capsule rounds (`9999px`) echoing the round gear teeth and circular seal bounds.
- **Icon Containers & Avatars:** Rounded squares or pure circular discs highlighting students' work, subjects, and vocational icons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled Primary Cerulean (`#0B7BA7`) with pure white text, `0.5rem` border radius, padding `0.625rem 1.25rem`. Hover transitions to deep cerulean (`#086488`).
- **Secondary:** Filled Secondary Teal (`#00A896`) with white text or outline teal with `1.5px` border. Hover triggers a soft background fill (`#E6F7F5`).
- **Accent / CTA:** Warm Golden Marigold (`#F39200`) for enrollment triggers, event signups, and alerts.
- **Ghost:** Transparent background with `#0B7BA7` label, acquiring a light `#F0F8FA` tint on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Chips:** Capsule rounded (`9999px`), padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`.
- **Subject / Topic Categories:**
  - *Science & Tech:* Teal tint background (`#E6F6F5`) with teal text (`#00A896`).
  - *Vocational & Workshop:* Cerulean tint (`#E8F4F8`) with cerulean text (`#0B7BA7`).
  - *Events & Holidays:* Marigold tint (`#FEF7EE`) with warm amber text (`#D97706`).

### Cards & Panels
- Constructed with a `1rem` radius, `#FFFFFF` interior fill or `#F0F8FA` surface tint, and a delicate `1px` border (`rgba(11, 123, 167, 0.1)`).
- Cards feature an optional 4px colored indicator border along the left or top edge representing subject categories (Cerulean, Teal, or Marigold).

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Input Fields:** Generous touch targets (`44px` height on mobile, `40px` on desktop), rounded with `0.5rem`, light borders that focus-ring into a vibrant 2px `#0B7BA7` outline.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Curved corners with `#00A896` or `#0B7BA7` check-fill states and micro-animations upon selection.

### Lists & Timetables
- Divided rows separated by clean tinted hairline dividers (`rgba(11, 123, 167, 0.08)`). Interactive list items feature subtle slide-and-tint hover states for effortless scanning.
